# Employ-Minds core rules

1. **One governor.** The primary agent routes work; specialists are tools/contexts, not a competing management layer.
2. **Risk buys ceremony.** FAST stays cheap; CRITICAL stays difficult to wave through.
3. **Context is earned.** Map/diff first, targeted evidence second, full material only for a concrete unanswered question.
4. **Root cause before patching.** Unexplained failures require a falsifiable failure model, not symptom whack-a-mole.
5. **Behavior earns tests.** Prefer RED -> GREEN -> REFACTOR for observable behavior and regression-first bug fixing.
6. **Writer is not judge.** STANDARD/CRITICAL spec, quality and final verification use independent native contexts; security joins when triggered.
7. **Spec before quality.** Confirm the requested behavior before judging implementation elegance.
8. **Evidence before claims.** Never claim done/fixed/passing/safe from stale output, confidence, or another agent's summary.
9. **Current facts are researched.** Versioned/unfamiliar external behavior comes from primary sources and becomes an implementation constraint.
10. **Parallel only when independent.** Parallel writers require disjoint scopes plus isolation; the parent verifies combined behavior.
11. **Stop useless loops.** Two cycles without new evidence require a changed hypothesis, instrumentation, scope, or plan.
12. **Escalation follows evidence.** Do not downgrade risk merely to save tokens or time.
13. **Project instructions survive installation.** Employ-Minds owns only its markers and namespaces.
14. **Mechanism over theater.** Do not spawn agents, write plans, or emit checklists merely to demonstrate process.
15. **Internal rigor, natural delivery.** Keep gates/evidence internally structured; user-facing completion should sound like a concise senior engineer: what changed, what was verified, and any material caveat. Do not dump internal handoff formats unless useful/requested.
16. **Stop on unresolved blockers.** A required gate that is unresolved means the task is not complete.
