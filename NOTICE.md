# Notices and upstream inspiration

Employ-Minds is an independently authored workflow harness. Its initial architecture synthesizes engineering ideas from two MIT-licensed projects:

- **Everything Claude Code (ECC)** — `affaan-m/ECC`, copyright (c) 2026 Affaan Mustafa.
- **Superpowers** — `obra/superpowers`, copyright (c) 2025 Jesse Vincent.

The upstream MIT license texts are preserved in `third_party/` for attribution and auditability. Exact revisions used for the initial synthesis are pinned in `UPSTREAMS.lock.json`.

Employ-Minds is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by either upstream project. The implementation intentionally consolidates overlapping workflow concepts behind one risk router instead of attempting to load both upstream frameworks as independent governors.
